01 — THE ASSESSMENT

A structured assessment of your operation

We walk the shopfloor, review your team, safety, data, reporting, workflow, layouts and processes against what we've seen work at scale, and hand you a clear, prioritized report, no vague "recommendations," just findings tied to specific corrective actions, cost of the change and potential cost savings. Things that will actually save money in the future. Our assessment is suitable for operations of any size - warehouse operations, depot operations, any production facility. It's not just about heavy automation or huge investments. Sometimes even a simple solution can unlock massive success.

Why structured this way

One paid step. One free-if-it-fails step.

The assessment carries a fee because it takes real time on-site and produces a concrete deliverable regardless of what happens next — that work has value whether or not you act on it.

The improvement work is priced differently on purpose. We want to be judged on results, not hours booked. If we lead a project and the savings don't materialise, you don't pay for our time — that risk sits with us, not you.
