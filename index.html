Why structured this way

One paid step. One free-if-it-fails step.

The assessment carries a fee because it takes real time on-site and produces a concrete deliverable regardless of what happens next — that work has value whether or not you act on it.

The improvement work is priced differently on purpose. We want to be judged on results, not hours booked. If we lead a project and the savings don't materialise, you don't pay for my time — that risk sits with us, not you.
