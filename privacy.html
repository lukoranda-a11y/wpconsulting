This notice explains what happens to your data when you visit this Well Pack Consulting page, and what the cookie preference on this site actually controls.

This site currently does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies of any kind. The only browser storage in use is a single, strictly necessary preference that remembers whether you've dismissed the cookie notice — this does not identify you and is not shared with anyone.

If that changes in the future (for example, if analytics is added to understand how the site is used), this page will be updated first, and the cookie banner will ask for your consent before anything non-essential runs.
